    How to play

    • Click or tap anywhere to walk there. Click a person, an object or a building to open its menu, just like The Sims. You can also use WASD or the arrow keys (Shift to run), or the joystick on a phone.
    • Everything you pick goes into your queue (bottom left). Your Sim walks over and does each thing in order. Tap a queued action to cancel it.
    • Walk into any venue: sit at tables, dance on the floor, order at the bar, use the toilet. Q / R or right-drag turns the camera; scroll or pinch to zoom.
    • Socials are grouped like The Sims: Friendly, Funny, Romantic, Mean, Mischief, Naija and Secrets. Friendship ❤️ and romance 💞 build separately, and how you feel changes how well things land.
    • Your Sim has six needs (Belle, Energy, Bladder, Hygiene, Fun, Social) and emotions made of moodlets. Confident Sims charm better; Flirty Sims flirt better; Tense or Angry ones… don't.
    • Skills grow as you do things: Charisma, Cooking, Dancing, Fitness, Photography and Hustle. Use Buy mode at home to upgrade your bed, TV, kitchen, power and more.
    • Time runs like a real day: 1 second is 1 game minute. Use ⏸ ▶ ▶▶ ▶▶▶ to pause or speed up. Activities fast-forward while they happen.
    • Places keep opening hours. Clubs open at 10pm, banks close at 4pm, owambes happen on Saturdays.
    • Map lets you walk there automatically or take a danfo, keke, okada or Bolt. Traffic can turn 12 minutes into 35.
    • Build 📱 Clout, 🤝🏾 Reputation and 🔗 Connections — reputation and clout are not the same thing.
    • Everybody has a secret, including you. Dig into people's stories, then keep, trade, expose or use what you learn. People remember what you did.
    • Your phone brings invitations, family requests, brand deals and drama. Say yes, then actually show up.
    • Victoria Island is the Luxury Zone: fit checks at the door, a DJ booth game, celebrity selfies, numbered drops and velvet ropes. Eat on credit and you become a Debtor until you hustle it back on the mainland.
    • Lekki Phase 1 (east, over the link bridge) is the social hub: Neon Palm, house parties on the close, friends inviting you out when you're broke, and chance encounters that end in romance… or trouble.
    • Watch your 😤 Stress. Flat drama, noisy neighbours, debt and fights push it up; at 70+ you earn and learn less.
    • Make it to crossover night on the 31st. Be at a church, club, rooftop, beach or home before midnight.

    Terms

    December Wahala is a free game for adults (18+). Be decent to other players: no harassment, hate, or real-world personal information in names. Secrets in the game are fictional and belong to game personas, not to real people. We may remove accounts that break these rules. The game is provided as-is.

    Privacy

    We store your name, username, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, your optional email, and your game progress. Other players can see your username, display name, character look, city, area, current in-game location and clout. We use one cookie to keep you signed in. No ads, no ad trackers.
