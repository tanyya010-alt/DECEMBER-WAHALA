      How to play

      • 📅 December has 31 days with 4 time slots each. Most things take one slot.
      • ⚡🍛🎉💬🧘 Keep your needs up. Your plumbob turns red when you're not okay.
      • 🚌 Moving around costs money and time. Lagos traffic is wicked. Trekking is free but slow.
      • ✈️ Family land from abroad on set days. Pick them up at the airport that same day or they'll remember it.
      • 💱 Dollars don't spend themselves. Change them at the mall BDC. The rate moves every day.
      • 🎊 Saturdays mean owambe. No aso-ebi? The aunties will notice.
      • 🎆 Make it to 31st December night for crossover. Memories, respect, clout and family love all count toward your score.
