    How to play

    • December has 31 days with four time slots each. Most things take one slot.
    • Tap a place on the map to travel there. Lagos traffic is wicked. Trekking is free but slow.
    • Keep ⚡ Energy, 🍛 Belle and 🔥 Vibes up. Build 📱 Clout, 🤝🏾 Reputation and 🔗 Connections.
    • Your persona has a superpower, a weakness and a secret. If your secret meter fills, you're exposed.
    • Everyone else has secrets too — the characters in town and the real players. Gist, investigate, then expose them or keep their secret.
    • Your fit matters. Each style "eats" in certain places. Change outfits at home.
    • Family land from abroad on set days. Pick them up at the airport.
    • Reach crossover night on the 31st. Hit your December goal for a big bonus.

    Terms

    December Wahala is a free game for adults (18+). Be decent to other players: no harassment, hate, or real-world personal information in names. Secrets in the game are fictional and belong to game personas, not to real people. We may remove accounts that break these rules. The game is provided as-is.

    Privacy

    We store your name, username, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, your optional email, and your game progress. Other players can see your username, display name, character look, city, area, current in-game location and clout. We use one cookie to keep you signed in. No ads, no ad trackers.
